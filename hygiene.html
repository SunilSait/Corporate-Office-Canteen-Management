Four Pillars

Pillars of Our Food Safety

Ingredient Sourcing

All ingredients sourced from FSSAI-approved suppliers with full traceability. Daily freshness audits on perishables.

Kitchen Sanitation

Deep cleaning protocols 3x daily. UV sanitization, pest control, and sterile food prep zones maintained round the clock.

Staff Health

Monthly health checkups for all kitchen staff. Mandatory hygiene training, PPE, and daily temperature screening.

Temperature Control

Cold chain maintained from procurement to storage. Hot foods kept above 65°C, cold items below 4°C at all times.
